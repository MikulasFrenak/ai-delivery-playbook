## What was built

`docs/adoption.md`, linked from the README. It lays out an enablement pipeline: research → workshop → pilot → shared conventions → independence. It also covers what makes adoption stick: review as mentoring (for humans and agents alike), fact-based feedback, decisions made through options and trade-offs (the same discipline as Research Before Implementing), and ownership when the team circles. It ends on the observation that most friction isn't technical.
